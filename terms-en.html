02 · What the Service Does

The service runs tests over your current network and provides results on network identity, IP address quality, network exits, connection protocols, domain resolution, service reachability, latency, and speed. It also supports local storage, import, and sharing of checkup reports.

Results help you understand current network conditions. Reachability tests reflect responses at the time of testing. They do not grant you access to third-party content or change any third party’s account, subscription, or regional requirements.

04 · Requests and Data Usage

Tests require an internet connection and send requests to test nodes and third-party sites. Speed tests continuously upload and download test data. They may consume substantial network data, battery power, and network resources, and affect other devices on the same network.

Consider your data plan, roaming status, and authorization to use the network before starting a test. Network access and data charges are governed by your agreement with the relevant provider.

If automatic checkups are enabled, the app may initiate tests when opened. You can disable automatic checkups in settings and stop a test in progress.

05 · Understanding Results

Results are observations made at a particular time, over a particular network path, and in a particular testing environment. They can be affected by your device, network provider, network accelerators, third-party site policies, load, and testing methods. Embedded webpages and native tools may use different measurement environments and return different results.

Inferred regions, risk scores, and grades are assessments based on available data. They may be inaccurate or incomplete and do not guarantee identity, location, security, or continued availability. Reports do not replace an independent security audit or professional judgment.

A failed, timed-out, or incomplete test does not mean a target service is permanently unavailable. Consider actual usage and repeated tests when interpreting results. Imported reports are supplied by the person sharing them; the service cannot establish their origin or verify that their contents have not been modified.

The service does not guarantee that third-party sites will always respond or that their content, interfaces, or regional rules will remain unchanged.

Local reports may be removed because of storage limits, deletion of the app, or changes to device data. Save any reports you wish to keep to a trusted location in advance. Deleting a local report does not recall copies already shared.
